A new luxury apartment on avenue Paul Cézanne

59 Cézanne is a confidential residence of new prestige apartments in Aix-en-Provence, on avenue Paul Cézanne in the Lauves district: private gardens, a heated pool and underground parking, with the Sainte-Victoire on the horizon. The last homes available are a 138 m² four-room apartment with a private garden and a 216 m² duplex with a private lift.

Where is 59 Cézanne?

At 59 avenue Paul Cézanne, Aix-en-Provence (13100), in the Lauves district. The city centre is about 4 minutes away by car and the A51 motorway about 3 minutes, according to the brochure. See the location.

Which homes are available?

A 138 m² four-room apartment with an 82 m² private garden, and a 216 m² duplex with a private lift. See the homes.

Is there a swimming pool?

A heated pool with a counter-current swimming system is planned according to the descriptive notice of 8 April 2025. To know what to check before buying, read our guide to apartments with a pool (in French).

How can I visit or receive the brochure?

Call 06 09 20 45 90 or write to us from the Contact page: visits are by appointment and the programme brochure is sent on request.

Can I follow the progress of the works?

Yes: drone photos and a drone video of the site are published on the Apartments page.

Why buy a new apartment in Aix-en-Provence?

Reduced notary fees, recent standards, finishes chosen from the plans: our guide to buying new (in French) sets out the steps, the legal guarantees and the costs, with official sources.
