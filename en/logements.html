Your questions

Frequently asked questions about 59 Cézanne

Where is the 59 Cézanne residence?

The 59 Cézanne residence is at 59 avenue Paul Cézanne, Aix-en-Provence (13100), France, in the Lauves district. According to the programme brochure, the city centre is about 4 minutes away by car, 8 minutes by bus or 20 minutes on foot, and the A51 motorway is 3 minutes away.

Which homes are still available at 59 Cézanne?

Two homes are still available at 59 Cézanne. The first is a 138 m² four-room apartment on the top floor (level 1, one level above the ground floor), with 3 bedrooms, a triple aspect, an 82 m² private garden, 2 underground parking spaces and a private cellar. The second is a 206 m² four-room duplex with 3 bedrooms including a master suite, over 100 m² of reception space, a private lift from the basement, a terrace and a private garden of about 56 m², 3 private parking spaces and a large cellar.

How much do the apartments at 59 Cézanne cost?

The 59 Cézanne website does not publish any price. The terms for the two available homes are discussed in person, by phone on +33 6 09 20 45 90 or during a visit.

When will 59 Cézanne be delivered, and how far along is the building work?

No delivery date is published for 59 Cézanne: the schedule is available on request, by message or by phone. The building work is documented: the excavation was dug and the foundation reinforcement was under way on 6 July 2026, the concrete walls were rising from the ground on 8 October 2026 and the roof was on by 11 November 2026. Dated photos and videos are published on the Apartments page.

Is there a swimming pool at 59 Cézanne?

Yes. The 59 Cézanne residence is planned with a heated communal pool with counter-current swimming and a sun deck with loungers, according to the descriptive notice of 8 April 2025. Access is by badge.

What shared facilities does the 59 Cézanne residence have?

59 Cézanne is a single building on two levels with canal-tile roofs. It has one underground parking level reached at street level from avenue Paul Cézanne, a lift accessible to people with reduced mobility, an enclosed and covered bicycle room, a heated pool and shared spaces planted with olive trees, almond trees, pines and lavender. The private gardens are at ground level or on the upper floor.

Do the apartments at 59 Cézanne have a view of Mont Sainte-Victoire?

Depending on the home, the view opens onto Mont Sainte-Victoire or onto the garden. The 138 m² apartment has an open view of the Sainte-Victoire to the east, and the living room, dining room and kitchen of the 206 m² duplex have large glazed bays facing the mountain.

Can I tour the 59 Cézanne homes in 3D?

Yes. The 59 Cézanne 3D tour page lets you explore the two available homes: a Residence view shows the whole building, the pool and the neighbourhood, a model shows each home from above, and a walk-through takes you room by room at eye level. These are computer-generated, non-contractual images based on the architect's sales plans; room areas are indicative, with a 5% tolerance.

Who is the developer of 59 Cézanne?

The developer of 59 Cézanne is PONTHIEU DH (formerly Ponthieu Développement Holding), a French simplified joint-stock company (SAS) based in Aix-en-Provence (La Cara, 418 ancienne route des Alpes, 13080), registered under SIREN 440 312 791. Founded in 2001 by Stéphane Ponthieu, the company works in property development and in the restoration of older buildings. It reports 84 completed projects and 420 homes delivered.

How can I visit 59 Cézanne or get the brochure?

Visits to 59 Cézanne are by appointment. The team answers on +33 6 09 20 45 90 from Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, and on Saturday by appointment; you can also write on WhatsApp, through the form on the Contact page or to ventes@ponthieu.fr. The programme brochure is sent on request.

How do you buy an apartment at 59 Cézanne?

Apartments at 59 Cézanne are sold off-plan under the French VEFA scheme; according to the reservation contract, a completion guarantee is to be lodged with the notary. Finishes can be personalised depending on construction progress, with floors in porcelain stoneware or parquet according to the descriptive notice. Notary fees are reduced on new builds.

What is near 59 Cézanne?

From 59 Cézanne, the programme brochure gives the following driving times: the hospital 7 minutes, the TGV station 15 minutes (Paris in about 3 hours), Marseille Provence international airport 22 minutes and Marseille 26 minutes. Bus lines 5 and 12 serve the city centre. On foot, the Lauves nursery school is 11 minutes away, the Atelier des Lauves (Cézanne's last studio) about 850 m and the Jardin des Peintres about 300 m.
